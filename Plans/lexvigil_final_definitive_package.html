Slide 1 of 7 — The Platform
Two legal AI problems. One evidence-grounded platform.
LexVigil is an AI Legal Intelligence Platform for India combining dispute research and contract contradiction detection into a single hybrid symbolic-AI system. It does not replace lawyers — it makes legal reasoning transparent, traceable, and accessible.
Track A — Dispute Intelligence
Describe a dispute in plain language. LexVigil extracts facts, classifies legal issues, retrieves verified statutes and precedents from BNS, BNSS, CPC, Consumer Act, Property, Family, Labour, and IT laws — then verifies every citation before showing it.
Track B — Contract Intelligence
Upload a PDF or DOCX contract. LexVigil resolves every internal reference, traces defined terms across amendments, builds a clause dependency graph, and detects contradictions across 12 conflict types — with a full evidence chain for every finding.
✦ Shared core: Both tracks feed the same Reasoning Core → Contradiction Detector → LLM Verifier pipeline. Every output carries source, evidence, confidence, and a professional-review recommendation.

Slide 2 of 7 — Two Real Problems
Legal research and contract analysis fail for the same fundamental reason: missing dependencies.
Problem A — Legal research
Users describe disputes in plain language. The legally relevant concepts appear under different statutory terminology (IPC → BNS, CrPC → BNSS). Identifying provisions, procedures, exceptions and precedents requires retrieval + reasoning, not just generation. Current tools either hallucinate citations or provide non-specific information.
User: "Landlord kept ₹80,000 deposit for 6 months" ↓ Naive LLM: "You can complain to consumer court" ↓ LexVigil: → Transfer of Property Act §108 → Consumer Protection Act 2019 → State Rent Control Act (Tamil Nadu) → Verified HC precedents on security deposit → Procedure: Legal notice → Consumer Commission
Problem B — Contract contradictions
A clause in §12.4 may silently conflict with a definition from §4.2 that was amended by §17.3. LLM-only review reads clauses sequentially without resolving dependencies — it misses multi-hop conflicts every time.
§4.2 "Payment Period" = 30 days ↓ defines §8.1 Pay within Payment Period ↓ amended by §17.3 Payment Period → 45 days ↓ not updated §12.4 Penalty after 30 days ← CONFLICT

Slide 5 of 7 — Track B: Contract Intelligence
12 contradiction types. Resolved dependencies. Auditable evidence chain.
Track B parses PDF/DOCX contracts into a structured clause dependency graph, resolves every cross-reference and defined term, propagates amendment effects through forward chaining, and reports candidate contradictions classified into 12 types.

Pass 3 → Amendments + exceptions
Pass 4 → Effective obligations
Pass 5 → Conflict generation
Pass 6 → Evidence assembly

Slide 6 of 7 — The Reasoning Core + AI Layer
Three-tier AI routing. Citation integrity by design. Evidence-first always.
The Reasoning Core is deterministic — it produces evidence-grounded candidates before any LLM call. The LLM verifies semantic ambiguity, explains findings, and provides natural language summaries. Citation integrity is a first-class engineering requirement.

Citation integrity rule (non-negotiable):
User question → retrieve candidate authority → validate source → extract relevant passage → LLM summarises verified passage → display citation + source URL. Never: LLM memory → invented case name.
Evidence output structure (every finding):
